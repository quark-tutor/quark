# Safety and privacy

This will be used by children, so these are requirements, not features.

- **Closed content.** Material comes only from approved sources. The kid-facing screens never link out to the open web.
- **Bounded "Ask".** Answers are written for the child's age. Sensitive topics (war, death, the body, anything frightening) get a gentle answer and "ask Mom or Dad." Every question is logged for parents.
- **No ads, no data sales.** Ever.
- **Minimal data.** Collect only what's needed to personalize: age, interests and activity. Keep it as close to the family as possible, ideally on their own device or account.
- **Parents in control.** Parents can see everything their child saw and asked, change the plan, and delete data.
- **Legal compliance.** Each family runs its own copy, which keeps children's privacy law simple. A copy serving other families must follow it in full (for example, COPPA in the US). See [Hosting and privacy](14-hosting-and-privacy.md).
- **Human review.** AI-generated explanations should be reviewable, and shared themes should be checked by people before others use them.
- **Values are visible.** A family's beliefs shape content only through a profile parents can read and edit. See [Family beliefs and values](09-family-values.md).
