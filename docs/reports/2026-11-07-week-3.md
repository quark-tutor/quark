- **Veterans Day is the second listen, Taps, not the second look.** A bugle call is a real music idea (a bugle has

  photograph would be a picture of war with less to teach.
- **Nellie Bly is fourth in then**, so she lands on Saturday 14 November, the day she set off in 1889.
- **A recording is offered only when it is public domain in the US**: a US government recording, one from 1925 or
  earlier, or CC0. A Commons "public domain" tag is not enough: a 1940 Berlin recording of the Pizzicato Polka is
