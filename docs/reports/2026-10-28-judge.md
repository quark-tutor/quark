1. **The migration is `20261029000000_attempt_verdict.sql`** (the brief's name is a digit short). It adds `verdict`,
   `judged_by` and `reasoning` to `attempts`, each checked against its values, and widens `model_runs.kind` to take

   are not.**
3. **`JUDGE_MODEL` is one line of code** in `core/wall/judge.ts`, as the tutor's voice is, not an environment variable:
   nothing is set in Vercel for it.
4. **The time limit is 3 seconds in all**, and a second try runs only when the first failed with a second or more left;
   past it the word rule judges. On the table about 1 answer in 18 went to the word rule. A longer limit would mean fewer
   of those and a longer wait; I kept the brief's.
5. **What the judge reads:** the card's words, the question, the accepted answers and the answer key's note on what
   counts, the hints shown so far, the grade band from the card's level (as the gate sends it), and the child's words.
   Wherever the card's words or the child's carry the nickname, it is sent as "the child", and a test holds that.
6. **An "answered the hint" verdict before any hint was shown counts as not yet.** A `not_a_guess` from the model writes
   nothing and says "Have a guess first.", as "I don't know" does now.
7. **The spend:** one `model_runs` row per answer judged (kind `judge`, counts and money only). `/parent/models` adds
   them up in a section of its own: this month, since the start, a call's cost, and a month for two boys at 40 answers a
   day. It does not list 40 rows a day under *Every run*. It also says whether Anthropic's key is set, as it does
   Google's.
8. **When the word rule judges in the model's place, the server logs one line,** the error's message alone and never
   the child's words, as the hear route does since 28 Oct.
9. **For the look on test:** test's week 1 runs from 21 to 27 Oct and is in review, so test has no card for the look's
