# Values packs: prior art

Nobody has defined a community-maintained, guardian-editable profile that controls when a topic comes up and from what perspective, for an AI that teaches children. The pieces exist in separate fields, and together they give us a vocabulary, a mechanism and a governance model to borrow. The research was done in October 2026.

## What exists, by field

| Field | Closest thing | What it defines | What we borrow |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AI alignment | [Values cards and the Moral Graph](https://meaningalignment.substack.com/p/the-first-moral-graph) (Meaning Alignment Institute, 2023) | A "values card" is a short value statement plus observable criteria for judging whether a response lives up to it. Cards are linked by context, and links are kept only when a sampled public agrees across opinion groups. | The card format: a value in plain words plus criteria a machine can check. Context-scoped rules. A consensus threshold for community edits. |
| AI alignment | [Value profiles](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.15484) (Google DeepMind, 2025) | A person's values written as a natural-language description that steers a model, rather than a numeric vector. Readable and editable by the person. | Keep the profile as readable text, never hidden weights. |
| AI alignment | [Pluralistic alignment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.05070) (Sorensen et al., 2024) | Names three modes: Overton (present all reasonable views), steerable (speak from a stated perspective), distributional. | "Several traditions fairly" is Overton mode. "Family tradition" is steerable mode. Use the terms. |
| AI alignment | [OpenAI Model Spec](https://model-spec.openai.com/2025-04-11.html) | A chain of command: platform rules nobody can override, then developer, then user, with each default marked as adjustable or not. | The layered override model: safety floor, then community pack, then family, then each guardian's blackball. The Spec never considers a child as the user with a guardian above them, which is our gap to fill. |
| AI alignment | [Collective Constitutional AI](https://www.anthropic.com/research/collective-constitutional-ai-aligning-a-language-model-with-public-input) (Anthropic, 2023) | About 1,000 people voted on statements; principles were kept only if they passed in both opinion groups. | A way to maintain a pack: members vote on statements, and a statement enters the pack only with broad agreement. |
| Enforcement | [gpt-oss-safeguard](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-oss-safeguard/), Llama Guard, [NeMo Guardrails](https://docs.nvidia.com/nemo/guardrails/) | A policy written in plain text is supplied at run time; the model labels content against it and explains why. NeMo's Colang is a small language for "topic X leads to action Y." | The execution layer. A pack serialized as the policy. Colang-style rules for the when-axis. |
| Media ratings | [Common Sense Media](https://commonsensemedia.org/about-us/our-mission/about-our-ratings), [Kids-In-Mind](https://kids-in-mind.com/about.htm), ESRB | Content tagged per category on a scale, separate from a single age threshold. Kids-In-Mind is deliberately a label, not a recommendation. | Describe, don't prescribe: the tutor tags each card, the pack sets the thresholds. Graded intensity within a topic. |
| Media filtering | [VidAngel](https://stephenfollows.com/vidangel-filter-objectionable-content-from-movies/), ClearPlay | Parents toggle fine-grained categories and the player skips tagged moments. Tags are community-sourced. | Per-item toggles with defaults set by the pack. Warning: VidAngel's sexual-orientation toggle is the kind of category that draws legal and ethical fire. |
| Media ratings | [Movieguide](https://www.movieguide.org/?p=1888), [Plugged In](https://www.pluggedin.com/?p=38589) | The only systems that rate the worldview of content, not just its incidents. Plugged In has a neutral "Spiritual Elements" bucket. | The idea of tagging a card's perspective. Warning: Movieguide's labels ("false religion") can't be shared vocabulary across packs. |
| Homeschool curriculum | [Cathy Duffy Reviews](https://cathyduffyreviews.com/search-advanced) | A faceted "religious perspective" field: Catholic, Protestant, Jewish, Humanist, secular, neutral, "secular but Christian-friendly," and so on, plus political perspective. | The closest existing vocabulary for a family's worldview. "Neutral" versus "secular but friendly" maps to omitted versus presented fairly. Gaps: no LDS, Muslim, Orthodox or Hindu entries. |
| Homeschool curriculum | [BookShark versus Sonlight](https://www.bookshark.com/blog/define-secular), [Timberdoodle](https://timberdoodle.com/blogs/td/is-timberdoodle-curriculum-religious) | The same curriculum sold in a Christian and a secular version. Origins is the pivotal fork. Timberdoodle names science, history and critical thinking as where worldview leaks in. | A subject-level map of where perspective matters. Proof that "omitted" is a real third option. |
| Teaching about religion | [First Amendment Center principles](https://www.cde.ca.gov/ci/HS/cf/documents/hssappendixf.pdf), [AAR guidelines](https://oasis2020.aarweb.org/sites/default/files/pdfs/About/Public_Affairs/RSNGuidelinesK-12PublicSchoolMay2010.pdf) | Academic not devotional; awareness not acceptance; expose not impose; inform not conform. Religions are internally diverse and change over time. | An operational definition of "several traditions fairly." Packs must be sub-tradition specific, since "Christian" is too broad to be one pack. |
| Law and governance | [UK religious education syllabus (SACRE)](https://dera.ioe.ac.uk/id/eprint/25293/2/CBP-7167_Redacted.pdf) | Each locality has a committee of faith groups, humanists, teachers and the council that agrees the syllabus. Parents can withdraw a child with no reason given. From 18, the young person decides. | The governance model for a community pack: a standing committee, not one author. No-reason withdrawal. An age at which the child's choice takes over. |
| Law and governance | [UK relationships and sex education guidance](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/68761d6b39d0452326e28e6f/RSHE_statutory_guidance_-_July_2025_.pdf) (2025) | A legally defined matrix: some topics can't be withdrawn from (relationships, health, safety), some can (sex education), with set ages and a rule that contested claims are presented as debate, not fact. | The matrix shape itself. A non-vetoable floor. Contested claims presented as contested. |
| Law and governance | [Mahmoud v. Taylor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahmoud_v._Taylor) (US Supreme Court, 2025) | Parents are entitled to notice and an opt-out when young children are exposed to normative messages on gender and sexuality, because young children can't separate exposure from endorsement. | Notice plus opt-out is now the legal baseline in the US. The dissent's worry about an unbounded topic list applies to us too. |
| Libraries | [ALA on labeling](https://www.ala.org/advocacy/intfreedom/librarybill/interpretations/labelingratingsystems) | Rating systems imposed by an institution violate intellectual freedom. Parents guide their own children's reading, and only theirs. | A warning that doubles as our rule: values controls are family-scoped, never imposed on anyone else's child. |

## What nothing covers yet

- A neutral, cross-tradition vocabulary for perspective. Every existing one is Christian-centric or built for one country.
- Topics beyond sex and religion. Death, origins, how history is framed and political framing have no descriptors anywhere.
- Conversational timing. Every system assumes fixed content. None handles a child asking a question the curriculum didn't raise.
- A multi-guardian veto and how to resolve conflicts.
- A non-vetoable safety floor. Only the UK sex-education rules hint at one.

## What this suggests for the pack format

1. **A pack is a set of values cards**, each a plain-language value plus criteria a machine can check, scoped to topics and ages.
2. **Three perspective modes with borrowed names:** family tradition (steerable), several traditions fairly (Overton, defined by the First Amendment Center principles), and omitted.
3. **A topic-by-age matrix** shaped like the UK guidance, with a non-vetoable floor at the bottom.
4. **Layered overrides** in the Model Spec style: floor, pack, family, guardian blackball.
5. **Packs maintained by a committee**, not an author, with statement voting and a consensus threshold, as SACRE and Collective Constitutional AI do.
6. **Sub-tradition specific.** Not "Christian" but Catholic, Latter-day Saint, Southern Baptist, and so on, because the traditions themselves say they're internally diverse.
7. **Family-scoped, always.** A pack shapes one family's tutor and never anyone else's.
