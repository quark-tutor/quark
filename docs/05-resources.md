# Resources to build on

Most of what this needs already exists and is free. It's just rarely pointed out to parents. The engine draws from these instead of making new content.

| Resource | What it is | Role in the system |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Core Knowledge Sequence](https://www.coreknowledge.org) | Grade-by-grade map of history, science, literature, art and music. Built to close knowledge gaps in underserved schools. | The long-term plan |
| [Ambleside Online](https://www.amblesideonline.org) | Year-by-year lists of great books, artists and composers (Charlotte Mason approach) | Read-alouds, artist and composer of the week |
| [Beast Academy](https://beastacademy.com) | Challenging math in comic form, about ages 6–13 | Math puzzles and a deeper track for older kids |
| [Story of the World](https://welltrainedmind.com) | World history told as stories, available on audio | Car audio, history spine |
| [Met Open Access](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection) and [Art Institute of Chicago](https://www.artic.edu/collection) | Hundreds of thousands of public-domain artworks in high resolution | Wall art, picture study |
| [NASA](https://www.nasa.gov) and [Smithsonian Learning Lab](https://learninglab.si.edu) | Real images, data and museum objects | Science and history cards |
| [LibriVox](https://librivox.org) and [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org) | Free public-domain audiobooks and books | Classic literature for audio and bedtime |
| [Musopen](https://musopen.org) | Free classical recordings | Composer of the week |
| [Libby](https://libbyapp.com) (public library) | Free ebooks and audiobooks with a library card | Modern books and audiobooks |
| [Junior Great Books](https://www.greatbooks.org) | Discussion-based reading of strong short works | A model for dinner questions |
| Kids' podcasts: Brains On, Greeking Out (Nat Geo Kids) | Science and mythology for ages 6–12 | Car audio |

> **License note:** Several of these are for families to use directly, not for this project to bundle. Core Knowledge and Khan Academy are NonCommercial. AmblesideOnline and Beast Academy can only be linked to. See [Prior art and building blocks](10-prior-art.md).

Know a great free resource? [Suggest it](../.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/resource-suggestion.md) by opening an issue.
