# Vision and goals

## The problem

Many capable kids aren't being stretched by school, and their parents don't have the time to fill the gap themselves. Asking parents to try harder won't solve it, because most have nothing left to give.

- **School often isn't enough.** Classwork rarely pushes kids to think about the world, big ideas or great books. In underserved districts, that gap widens with every grade.
- **The good material exists, but nobody hands it over.** Most of the world's art, music, literature and science is online and free. Teachers rarely point parents to the best curated sources, and few parents have time to find and organize them.
- **Parents' time is limited.** Work, after-school activities and everyday logistics fill the week.
- **Plans that need follow-up fade.** A reading list or a schedule works for a few weeks, then life gets busy and it stops.
- **The competition is built to win.** Apps and videos are designed to grab attention, not to raise well-rounded kids. Good material loses unless something is working on its side.

## Goals

Success means kids pick up real knowledge and real curiosity year after year, without parents having to push it every day.

1. **Broad, deep knowledge.** By middle school, kids recognize great art, music, books, scientific ideas and the major stories of history. They know them as familiar things, not as test answers.
2. **Curiosity that drives itself.** Kids explore on their own because it's interesting, not because they were told to.
3. **Challenging but age-appropriate.** Material sits a step above where each child is. Their pace and interests decide how hard it gets, not their grade.
4. **Easy for parents to keep up.** It keeps going through busy seasons with about 10 minutes a week from a parent.
5. **Better conversations at home.** It gives families good things to talk about at dinner and in the car, so parents stay the teachers who matter most.
6. **Safe and calm by design.** No ads, no open web, no endless feed and nothing designed to keep kids hooked.

## What this is not

- Not a replacement for school or for parents.
- Not a hardware business. Any screen should work.
- Not another app competing for screen time. It's built to hand kids back to real life.
