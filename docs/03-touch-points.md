# Touch points

The wall screen is the anchor. The other touch points use time families already spend together, so none of them need a new block in the schedule.

| Touch point | Where and when | What it delivers | Who drives it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Wonder Wall** | Touchscreen in the kitchen or hallway, open anytime | Something new every day (a painting, a science idea, music, history on a map, a puzzle), each 3–4 layers deep. An "Ask" button for kid-safe questions. Per-child profiles and explorer stamps. | Kids, on their own |
| **Ambient art and music** | TV or frame in art mode, a speaker at breakfast | Artist of the week on the wall, composer of the week playing | Nobody, it just runs |
| **Car audio** | Drives to school and activities | A queued audiobook, story-history or kids' science podcast | Kids pick from a short approved list |
| **Dinner question** | Dinner table, 5 minutes | One question tied to the week's theme, plus a few facts for parents | Parents |
| **Bedtime read-aloud** | 20 minutes, most nights | A great book read 2–3 grade levels above the child's own reading level | Parents |
| **Weekend hour** | Saturday or Sunday | One hands-on activity: an experiment, a museum, a build or a nature walk | Parents, with a ready-made plan |
| **Bookshelf** | The child's room | A few rotating library picks that match the theme | The tutor picks, parents fetch |
| **Parent digest** | Weekly, by email or phone | What each child explored and asked, next week's plan, and three dinner questions. Reply to steer it. | Tutor to parents |

## Wonder Wall: first sketch

- Locked to one app (for example, Guided Access on an iPad, or a kiosk browser). No way out to the open web.
- Home screen shows today's five cards: Look (art), Wonder (science), Listen (music), Then (history on a map), Puzzle.
- Each card goes 3–4 layers deeper on tap, then ends with a question to take to a person.
- Each child has a profile, with explanations written at their level.
- Explorer passport: stamps for eras, artists and ideas they've explored.
